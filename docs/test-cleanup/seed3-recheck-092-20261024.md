## What this does and does not establish

The run confirms the exact repeated-target/current-plan branch and its successful downstream ERP
effect. It does not establish a stable reduction in total latency, calls, or cost. A regression
would be shown by the same-frame proposal being cleared after repeated-target bookkeeping, another
full `screen_decision` being required before the new-record click, or the retained proposal being
compiled against a different observation or target.
